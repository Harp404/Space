# AstroMesh — shot list

*Pairs with `NARRATION.md`. Seven scenes, about two and a half minutes.*

---

## Pre-flight

1. Gateway and frontend running.
2. Browser **1600×900**, zoom **100%**, bookmarks hidden.
3. Open **HOW** once, then close it — warms the corridor and ground layers.
4. Open **FILTERS**, tick *Show all tracked objects*, wait, untick — warms the GPU screen.
5. Reload for a clean boot.
6. One dry run **with wifi off**.

---

## 1 · Open — 0:00

**Do** nothing; let the boot and fly-in play.
**Screen** globe right, `BLOCKED 44%` left.
**Watch** don't talk over the boot bar.

## 2 · The refusal — 0:15

**Do** **RISK** → **VOTE** on the top card.
**Screen** toast `OPERATOR VOTE REFUSED`; rulebook fills with red ✕ and grey ○ rows.
**Point at** the `FR-07` row and its **NO OVERRIDE** tag, then the grey rows.
**Watch** if the top card holds a commandable object the reason differs — **read the screen**, not the script. The point holds either way.

> The thesis scene. Slow down here.

## 3 · The chain — 0:45

**Do** **HOW**. Globe flies to the corridor. Scroll the panel at reading speed.
**Screen** red ribbon Spain → India, DINOv3 ground strip, 12 numbered steps.
**Pause on** step 3 (density), 5 (DINOv3), 8 (Ec 19.7×), **9 (the DAS comparison)**.
**Watch** if you cut anything in the film, not step 9.

## 4 · Ride it down — 1:25

**Do** **⏵ FOLLOW DESCENT**, ~12 s. Optionally **¼×** over land. Then **✕ STOP**.
**Screen** camera falls with the stage; HUD counts altitude, flight-path angle, downrange.
**Watch** say the "kinematic replay" line while that caption is visible.

## 5 · The storm — 1:50

**Do** **STORMS** → **⚡ REPLAY GANNON**. Then **LIVE** to restore.
**Screen** three bands paint; header `Kp 9 · G5`; `3/8 ground stations reachable`; map key bottom-left.
**Point at** the station count, then the key.
**Watch** the LIVE restore is worth two seconds — the before/after sells it.

## 6 · Theme independence — 2:10

**Do** **STORMS** → scroll to **THEME INDEPENDENCE** → **PROVE IT**.
**Screen** five release-gate scenarios, all four badges, `engine changes required: 0`.
**Watch** aircraft dispatch is API-side. Mention only if asked; don't hunt for it on camera.

## 7 · GPU and close — 2:25

**Do** **FILTERS** → **⚡ GPU SCREEN**. Close filters. **HOW** to end on the signal.
**Screen** objects, pairs, milliseconds, pair-checks per second; candidate lines flash.
**Watch** no WebGPU means it says so plainly — skip to the close, that's a graceful degrade.

---

## Spare 60 seconds

1. **Kill quorum.** **FLEET** → **STOP** two nodes. Gate slams to BLOCKED.
   *"Flight Rule eight is non-negotiable. I just removed this system's authority in four seconds."*
2. **Recourse.** In **HOW**, an unresolved step's acquisition buttons.
   *"It computes the cheapest set of measurements that would clear this — and verifies no smaller set works."*
3. **Agent.** **MISSION** → **ACTIVATE**, watch the feed.
   *"It plans a real manoeuvre, then submits itself to the same gate — and stands down when refused."*
4. **Receipts.** Bottom of **HOW**.
   *"Hash-chained, replays byte-for-byte. Change one input and the hash changes."*

## Round 1 — one breath, anywhere

> *"Everything from Round 1 is still here — the catalogue, the screening, the avoidance planner, launch windows, multi-operator consensus, the mission-control chat. Round 2 put a gate in front of every irreversible thing it can do."*

---

## Notes

- One take **per scene**. Cut between them.
- Don't narrate clicking. Click, then speak to what appeared.
- When the gate refuses, **don't apologise** — that is the product working.
- Never say "formally verified" unqualified. Say what was checked, to what bounds.
- Keep `demo/astromesh-round2.mp4` cued as the fallback.
